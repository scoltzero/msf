# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Operators and maintainers of self-hosted MSF installations who manage authorized DNS and proxy infrastructure across Linux, Docker, fnOS, Unraid, and macOS environments.

## Product Purpose

Provide one auditable interface for configuring and operating MosDNS, Mihomo, routing rules, clients, logs, updates, and system diagnostics without hiding the underlying service state.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and technically confident. The interface should feel polished and modern while remaining an operational tool first.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, high-saturation decorative effects, motion that competes with live system data, inconsistent component styling, and visual changes that replace the established MSF identity.

## Design Principles

- Preserve operational clarity and make service state immediately legible.
- Use visual effects as environmental material, never as a substitute for hierarchy.
- Keep the MSF theme, brand assets, and interaction patterns consistent across modules.
- Prefer evidence-backed status and predictable controls over decorative novelty.
- Scale gracefully from desktop administration to constrained screens.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, keyboard-visible focus, reduced-motion behavior, reduced-transparency fallbacks, and color-independent state communication.
